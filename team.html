Our Team

Meet the passionate people dedicated to making Less Fortunate Charity's mission a reality every day

Executive Leadership

Our leadership team brings together decades of combined experience in community development, social work, education, and nonprofit management.

PD

Patrickson Durand

Principal Officer & Founder

✉ in

Patrickson founded Less Fortunate Charity in 2023 with a conviction that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, and support. With deep roots in the South Shore neighborhood, he has dedicated his life to closing the opportunity gap for underserved Chicagoans. He oversees organizational strategy, program development, and community partnerships.

DM

Diana Martinez

Executive Director

✉ in

Diana brings over 12 years of experience in nonprofit administration and program management. She oversees day-to-day operations, grant compliance, and team coordination. A graduate of DePaul University, Diana is passionate about building sustainable systems that create lasting community change.

TW

Thomas Williams

Director of Programs

✉ in

Thomas leads the design and delivery of all our community programs. With a background in social work and community organizing, he ensures our interventions are evidence-based, equitable, and responsive to the evolving needs of Chicago's most vulnerable residents.

Our Staff

A dedicated team of professionals who show up every day to support our programs and the people we serve.

SC

Sandra Chen

Community Outreach Coordinator

Sandra manages our community partnerships, outreach events, and volunteer recruitment. She is fluent in Mandarin and Spanish, enabling us to connect with a broader range of community members.

RJ

Robert Johnson

Education Program Manager

Robert oversees our scholarship program and tutoring initiatives. A former Chicago Public Schools teacher with 8 years of classroom experience, he brings both heart and expertise to our education work.

AP

Amara Phillips

Case Manager & Client Services

Amara is the first point of contact for families seeking emergency assistance. She guides clients through our intake process with compassion and efficiency, connecting them to the right resources quickly.

KL

Kevin Lewis

Job Training Program Lead

Kevin coordinates our career readiness and job placement programs. With a background in human resources and workforce development, he builds employer relationships that open doors for program participants.

FO

Faith Owusu

Finance & Operations Associate

Faith ensures the financial integrity and operational efficiency of the organization. She manages bookkeeping, vendor relationships, and reporting — keeping us transparent and accountable to our donors.

MR

Miguel Reyes

Communications & Digital Coordinator

Miguel manages our digital presence, storytelling, and donor communications. His work amplifies the voices of our community and helps us grow our reach and impact online.

Board of Directors

LN

HG

Evelyn Park
