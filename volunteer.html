Volunteer With Us

Your time and talents can change lives — join our community of dedicated volunteers

Make a Real Difference

Volunteers are the backbone of Less Fortunate Charity. Without the generous contribution of time and skills from people like you, we simply could not serve the hundreds of families and individuals who depend on our programs each year.

Whether you have a few hours a month or a few hours a week, there is a meaningful role for you here. Our volunteers come from all walks of life and bring a wide range of skills — all united by a shared desire to lift others up.

Benefits of Volunteering

  • ✓ Make a tangible impact in the Chicago community
  • ✓ Build new skills and gain valuable experience
  • ✓ Receive volunteer training and ongoing support
  • ✓ Earn a certificate of service for your records
  • ✓ Connect with like-minded community members
  • ✓ Flexible scheduling to fit your lifestyle

Volunteer at a Glance

Minimum Age: 16 years old (under 18 with parental consent)

Commitment: As little as 2 hours/month

Training: Free orientation provided for all volunteers

Background Check: Required for roles working directly with youth or vulnerable adults

Location: 2132 E 72nd St, Chicago, IL 60649 (and remote options available)

Open Volunteer Roles

Tutor & Academic Mentor

Help K–12 students with homework, reading, and college prep. No teaching certification required — just patience and a love of learning.

2–4 hrs/week

Food Distribution Assistant

Help sort, pack, and distribute food donations at our community food drives and distribution events held throughout the year.

Event-based
💼

Career Coach

Share your professional expertise to help job seekers with resume writing, interview prep, and career guidance.

1–2 hrs/week
📣

Community Outreach Volunteer

Help spread awareness about our programs by attending community events, distributing materials, and engaging with local organizations.

Flexible
💻

Digital & Admin Support

Assist with social media, data entry, grant research, or website updates. Many of these roles can be done remotely from home.

Remote friendly

Event Organizer

Help plan and run fundraising events, community celebrations, and awareness campaigns throughout the year.

Project-based
🏥

Health & Wellness Aide

Monthly events

Peer Support Advocate

2 hrs/week
