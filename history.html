Our History

How a neighborhood commitment became a recognized nonprofit

The Beginning

Built on a Simple Belief

Less Fortunate Charity was born from a straightforward conviction: neighbors should look out for one another. Founded by Patrickson Durand at 2132 East 72nd Street on Chicago's South Side, the organization started small — a few people, a few resources, and an unwavering commitment to the families around them.

What began as informal assistance to community members in need gradually took shape as a structured nonprofit — one with programs, volunteers, donors, and measurable impact. Today, Less Fortunate Charity holds 501(c)(3) federal tax-exempt status (EIN: 99-3459999) and continues to grow its reach across Chicago.

Milestones

Our Journey

START
Founding

Patrickson Durand Establishes Less Fortunate Charity

At 2132 East 72nd Street in Chicago, Patrickson Durand formally establishes Less Fortunate Charity. The organization begins with a simple mission: to meet the immediate needs of less fortunate individuals and families on Chicago's South Side.

FOOD
First Programs

Emergency Food Pantry & Crisis Assistance Launch

The organization launches its first formal program — an emergency food pantry providing grocery assistance to families in crisis. Utility support and emergency financial grants soon follow, responding to the community's most urgent needs.

IRS
501(c)(3) Recognition

Federal Tax-Exempt Status Granted

Less Fortunate Charity receives IRS recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity (EIN: 99-3459999). This milestone enables donors to contribute with full tax deductibility and opens the door to grant funding and institutional partnerships.

EDU
Programs Expand

Education Support Program Launched

Recognizing that emergency aid alone cannot break cycles of poverty, the organization launches its Education Support Program — providing tutoring, school supplies, scholarships, and academic mentorship to K–12 students in the community.

JOBS
Career Development

Job Training & Career Development Program Added

The Job Training program launches, empowering adults with resume writing, interview coaching, and job placement support. Within its first cohort, 82% of participants secure employment within six months — a benchmark the program maintains to this day.

NOW
Today

500+ Lives Touched & Growing

Less Fortunate Charity now operates five active programs, serves 100+ families annually, and relies on a growing network of volunteers and donors. From a single address on East 72nd Street, the organization continues to expand its reach and deepen its impact across Chicago.
