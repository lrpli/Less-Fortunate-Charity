Darnell: From Surviving to Thriving

"Before Less Fortunate Charity, I had given up on ever finding stable work. Now I have a full-time job, my own apartment, and I'm saving money for the first time in my life."

Darnell came to us in early 2025 after losing his job during an economic downturn. With two children depending on him and an eviction notice on his door, he was at a breaking point. Through our Emergency Assistance program, we helped stabilize his housing. He then enrolled in our 12-week Job Readiness Workshop.

Within five months of completing the program, Darnell secured a full-time position as a facilities coordinator earning a living wage. He is now one of our volunteer mentors, helping others navigate the same path he walked.

Education Support Program

Kezia: First in Her Family to Go to College

"I never thought college was possible for someone like me. The scholarship and mentorship I received changed everything — not just for me, but for my whole family."

Kezia grew up in a household where making ends meet was a constant struggle. A straight-A student with enormous potential but no financial pathway to higher education, she nearly resigned herself to bypassing college entirely.

A school counselor connected her to our Education Support Program. Kezia received a full scholarship, one-on-one college application coaching, and an academic mentor. She was accepted to three universities and chose to study nursing — a career she says will let her give back to her community the way we gave back to her.

K
Kezia A.
Scholarship Recipient, 2025  ·  Enrolled at University of Illinois Chicago

Emergency Assistance

The Rivera Family: Kept Together When It Mattered Most

"We had three days before we would have been on the street. One phone call to Less Fortunate Charity changed everything for us."

Maria and Carlos Rivera contacted us in desperation — a medical emergency had wiped out their savings, and they were three months behind on rent with two young children and a third on the way. Their landlord had already filed for eviction.

Within 48 hours, our case manager connected them with emergency rental assistance, a local food pantry, and a healthcare navigator to help manage Carlos's ongoing medical bills. The family remained housed. Six months later, they are financially stable, and Maria has enrolled in our job training program to increase the family's income.

R
The Rivera Family
Emergency Assistance Recipients, 2025  ·  Chicago, IL
🧘 Health & Wellness
Health & Wellness Program

Tamika: Finding Support She Didn't Know Existed

"I had been suffering in silence for years. At the health fair, someone finally listened — and pointed me toward resources that actually helped."

Tamika had been experiencing symptoms of depression and anxiety for several years but lacked both health insurance and knowledge of available resources. She attended our Community Health Fair on a whim, expecting it to be just about physical health checkups.

Instead, she connected with a mental health outreach worker who helped her access free counseling through a partner organization, enroll in Medicaid, and join a weekly wellness group. Tamika says it was the first time in years she felt hopeful. She is now a regular volunteer at our health events.

T
Tamika B.
Health Program Participant, 2025  ·  Now a Health Volunteer
