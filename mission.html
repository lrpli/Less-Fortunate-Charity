Mission & Vision

What we believe, what we're building, and why it matters

Our Mission

"To provide essential resources, emergency assistance, and long-term opportunity to individuals and families facing hardship in Chicago."

Less Fortunate Charity was founded on the belief that every person deserves dignity, support, and a fair chance. Located in the heart of Chicago's South Side, we walk alongside our neighbors through crises, through education, and through every step toward a more stable future.

Our Vision

A More Equitable Chicago

We envision a Chicago where socioeconomic circumstances do not determine a person's potential — where every resident, regardless of their background or hardship, has access to food, shelter, education, and employment opportunities. Our vision is not charity for charity's sake, but a community where neighbors truly lift one another up.

What Guides Us

Core Values

These principles shape every program we run, every dollar we spend, and every person we serve.

❤️

Compassion

We approach every interaction with genuine empathy and respect, honoring the dignity of each person who walks through our door.

🔍

Transparency

We operate with full accountability. Our finances, programs, and outcomes are open to our donors and to the communities we serve.

🌍

Inclusivity

We serve all people without discrimination — regardless of background, religion, national origin, or immigration status.

📈

Impact

We measure success by real outcomes: families stabilized, students supported, jobs secured. Good intentions are not enough.

🏘️

Community

We are rooted in the neighborhoods we serve. Local input and community partnerships drive our priorities and our work.

⚖️

Integrity

Every dollar donated is stewarded with care. We do what we say, and we say what we do — always.
