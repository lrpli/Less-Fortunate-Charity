Partners & Sponsors

Our work is made possible by the generous organizations and businesses that stand with us

🏅 Gold Partners
🏦

Chicago Community Trust

Foundation

A cornerstone funder of our Education Support and Emergency Assistance programs. Their investment has helped us serve hundreds of Chicago families since 2023.

"Less Fortunate Charity exemplifies community-led change at its best."

🏢

South Side Business Alliance

Corporate Partner

Provides job placement connections for our Job Training program graduates, sponsoring our annual career fair and employing over 30 of our program alumni.

"Our members are proud to hire graduates from this outstanding program."

🎗️

United Way of Metropolitan Chicago

Nonprofit Alliance

A vital partner in our emergency assistance referral network. United Way connects us to additional resources and amplifies our reach throughout the Chicagoland area.

"They are one of the most impactful organizations we fund in the South Side."

🎓

Chicago Public Schools

Education Partner

🌿

Greater Chicago Food Depository

Food Security Partner

Supplies food for our distributions and food drives, helping us provide nutritious meals to families experiencing food insecurity across the South Side.

⚖️

Legal Aid Chicago

Legal Services Partner

Provides pro bono legal consultations for families navigating evictions, benefits disputes, and other legal challenges through our emergency assistance program.

Why Partner With Us

Partnering with Less Fortunate Charity is more than a donation — it is a meaningful, visible commitment to Chicago's most underserved communities.

🎯

Proven Impact

Your partnership funds programs with documented, measurable outcomes — 82% job placement, 100% scholarship college enrollment, hundreds of families stabilized.

📣

Brand Visibility

Gold and Silver partners receive logo placement on our website, event materials, social media shoutouts, and our annual impact report distributed to 2,000+ stakeholders.

💰

Tax Benefits

All corporate donations are tax-deductible. We are a 501(c)(3) registered nonprofit (EIN: 99-3459999) and provide formal acknowledgment letters for every gift.

🤝

Employee Engagement

Bring your team closer together through volunteer days, skills-based volunteering, and matching gift programs that amplify your employees' own giving.

🌍

Community Goodwill

Demonstrate that your organization cares about the neighborhoods where you operate. Community trust and goodwill are among the most valuable assets a brand can build.

📊

Transparent Reporting

Partners receive quarterly impact updates and a year-end report detailing exactly how their contribution was used and what outcomes it produced.
